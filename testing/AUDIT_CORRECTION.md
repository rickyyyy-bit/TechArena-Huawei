# Audit correction — numerical action metadata

30 October 2026. The earlier audit incorrectly treated the handbook's directional tables as the complete source of action-effect information. Our official returned observations also expose `choices[].status_updates`, alongside the action description and Energy cost. This was verified in preserved original-v3, monthly-candidate, risk-01 and risk-02 outputs. No simulator package, dataset, database or private state was inspected.

**A numerical prediction matching the contemporaneous offered metadata is not an invented rule merely because the handbook omits that amount.** The earlier blanket finding requires qualification. Original reports, transcripts and historical summaries are preserved unchanged; this separate addendum corrects their interpretation rather than rewriting evidence. See the [checked-record references and hashes](results/action-metadata-audit-addendum.json).

Three things must remain separate:

1. An offered update is current evidence about the action, not a permanent formula for every future observation.
2. It does not prove that the next returned statistic actually changed by that amount. Check the fresh result before claiming an achieved gain or spending based on changed state. Unchanged visible results remain observed lack of visible change, not automatically missing evidence or proof of no possible benefit.
3. A risk-reducing update does not reveal the total hidden state, a failure threshold or verified clearance of every concern. An attempt, comfortable visible statistics or reassuring HR feedback cannot establish blanket safety.

This correction also matters for comparisons: offered amounts may be compared within the same statistic, with their actual Energy costs, while unrelated statistics still cannot be ranked as a shared numerical scale. The metadata does not publish promotion weights, a universal promotion cutoff, a generic visibility-point statistic or a safety guarantee.

The genuine risk-01 main-action omission remains: prior Energy mitigation often replaced a comparison of the currently offered main action, or the alternative was discounted using Health alone while its risk-reduction direction was omitted. Risk-02 makes those directions more visible, but premature clearance, unsupported causal timing and dismissal of uneasy feedback remain separate concerns. Those findings must be judged against actual returned context, not against keywords or the mere presence of numbers.

New records have been corrected. The gameplay instructions already allow numbers supplied by the current interface; no strategy was changed during a test and no additional numerical rule or target is introduced by this correction.
