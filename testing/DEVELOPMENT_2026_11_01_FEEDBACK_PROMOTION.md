## Recurring execution defects

1. Advertised benefits are repeatedly substituted for observed gains. The mixed run's month-18 reports showed Output 5 → 6 → 6 → 6, yet notes continued to justify maximum Output. Across multiple historical versions, returned absent intended gains recur. Inventory entries are review leads, not automatically wasted actions: projects may still deliver Skill, and protection may lack a visible-stat increase.
2. Seven recoveries at Health 10 in the 69.03 run claimed buffer maintenance. They consumed seven of 48 Energy without the claimed visible gain. Below-cap recovery remains vital: verified earlier Health-zero failures occurred at months 3 and 12.
3. The 62.48 rerun had Skill 108 versus 110, Network 54 versus 33, but risk contribution 3 versus 10 and Wealth 27.9 versus 39.1. Its score gap is mainly risk and Wealth, not inadequate ending Skill. It used no defusal or protective main action despite serious warnings. Existing protection principles must remain; this revision does not claim those failures are solved.
4. Unsupported claims include Skill being maxed at 100, safety inferred from one Health reading, and mistaken final-review timing. The new actual-benefit wording removes invented Skill saturation; existing Health, warning and horizon safeguards are retained without a broad rewrite.

## Preservation checklist and manual review

| Case / invariant | Review result |
| --- | --- |
| Health 10 with useful alternative | Compare another legal affordable benefit; no imaginary buffer or compulsory Output. |
| Health 10, no worthwhile alternative | Explain unused Energy; don't force spending. |
| Recurrent depletion below 10 | Further useful recovery remains available; no safe threshold or quota. |
| Warning persists after defusal | Original concern summary and Energy/main-action protection comparisons remain intact. |
| Urgent Health and organisational threats | Original urgency comparison and two-point affordability retained; no automatic priority based on raw units. |
| Observed absent gain | Explicit next-choice reassessment; repeating requires evidence or another purpose. |
| Missing feedback | Unknown, not zero or success; current observation only when consequential. |
| Mitigation without visible gain | Supported protection retains value; not a blanket stop rule. |
| Project yields Skill only | Credit actual capability; compare alternatives without inventing Output/Network gains. |
| Strong review without promotion | Grade and advancement separated; reassess development without promises or thresholds. |
| Broad weak-review feedback | Keep cause uncertain; reporting is not completed work. |
| Financial distress | Original affordability and no-future-rescue rules preserved. |
| Useful capability without warning | Existing responsibility-based preparation retained; no raw Skill cap or automatic learning. |
| Last review settled / ending returned | Original horizon and stop rules retained; no new fixed month schedule. |

Default development priorities, main-action alternatives, Dignity protection, current-phase legality, quarterly three-point budget and financial/Health/risk safeguards are retained. This is instruction desk review, not model adherence testing. No exact historical choice sequence or numerical readiness target was encoded.
