# Quarterly planning

## Purpose and use

Turn the published quarterly actions into a sustainable career plan. Use this Skill when the official observation enters an Energy-action phase or offers a quarterly main action. Use performance-review feedback to update the next plan. One agent chooses and submits each action.

## Observe

Read the legal actions and remaining Energy from the current observation, plus Health, Dignity, Skill, Network, Output, Wealth, Level, CurrentLevelDuration, warnings, recent changes, and the latest review if available. The public rules state that a quarter grants 3 Energy, unused Energy does not carry forward, and one main action or no main action is allowed. Check the actual current observation before every expenditure.

The handbook's normal sequence is quarterly Energy, main action, settlement and warnings, then any six-month review and bonus. Follow the actual returned phase; a month number or story mentioning an appraisal does not itself prove that a formal review has settled. Identify which reviews and promotion windows have actually settled and which remain. Output supports reviews and promotion; it is not a separate published ending dimension. After the last review and promotion opportunity has actually settled, do not justify more Output solely by an unavailable future review. Compare benefits that can still reach the ending, including wellbeing, Wealth, Skill, Network, and risk control.

## Decision checks before development

Apply these checks before the development preference or a provisional Energy plan:

- **Sustainable Health:** compare current Health with recent losses, repeated returns to a fragile state, optional commitments and the decisions before the next actual recovery opportunity. A single reading, the midpoint, or a recent recovery does not establish safety. When recent depletion leaves continued strain concerning, compare further useful recovery and the offered protective main action before marginal growth. Keep competing financial, performance and organisational threats in the comparison. No fixed Health target or assumed future rescue is permitted.
- **Actual benefit before the next choice:** compare the intended benefit with the returned result. If the relevant value increased, count only that observed gain. If it was observed unchanged, do not buy the same action again for that gain unless relevant evidence changed or another useful purpose is independently supported. Compare an alternative now; unchanged advertised effects are not new evidence. If the result is unavailable, keep the gain unknown rather than treating it as absent or achieved; obtain a current observation only when needed to choose legally or resolve a consequential uncertainty. Retain useful Skill gains even when a project's intended Output or Network gain did not appear. Supported risk mitigation can remain useful without a visible-stat increase. Do not invent hidden caps, delayed effects or engine faults.
- **Supported diagnosis:** a weak review can support several possible causes. Identify what the feedback actually establishes, compare useful work completion with visibility, and keep the diagnosis uncertain where necessary. Do not label a raw Skill value sufficient for a level without supporting task/review evidence.

## Default development emphasis and public action effects

**Risk control before discretionary growth; Output, Network, and Dignity before more Skill-only investment when no capability deficit is supported.** High visible statistics do not establish organisational safety. Retain Health, financial, performance, and capability checks; they can also threaten survival. This preference implements balanced development and does not assert unpublished performance weights.

Use the published Energy actions as the starting comparison:

| Energy action | Cost | Public direction |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Prepare report materials | 1 | Output +, Dignity + |
| Network maintenance | 1 | Network +, Dignity + |
| Mental recovery | 1 | Dignity + |
| Health recovery | 1 | Health + |
| Defuse legacy risk | 2 | No published direct statistical effect |
| High-intensity work | 2 | Output +, Health -, Dignity - |
| Skill learning | 2 | Skill +, Health - |

In a stable quarter without a supported capability gap, consider report preparation and Network maintenance first, subject to the decision checks above. Their public directions are initial predictions; contrary returned results change their priority. Use current feedback to allocate between delivery and relationships, and compare recovery or risk mitigation when needed. Compare combined benefits with Skill-only growth and intense work using current offered metadata and actual results; do not assume numeric returns or force a fixed allocation. Mental recovery can address a Dignity need when the combined actions do not adequately address it. Do not repeatedly spend for Dignity already at its upper bound while ignoring another consequential need.

For the main action, compare **Report results to your manager** as the first development candidate only when present needs and results support it: its public directions are Output +, Network +, Dignity +. Compare it with a key project (Output +, Skill +, Network +), showcasing (Output +, Dignity +), relationship repair (Network +, Dignity +), and other offered actions. Always include the offered protective action when Health depletion or organisational risk remains a concern, even after Energy recovery. Select according to actual need, observed effectiveness, workload and warnings; reporting is not automatically best. Do not assume an unlisted effect, that reporting alone creates completed work, or that vague performance feedback establishes a visibility-only problem.

Reviews and promotion consider multiple factors, including Skill, Network, and Output for promotion; exact formulas are not published. Weak delivery or collaboration warrants correction even with high Skill. Treat repeated weak performance as a survival concern, without claiming that a particular Output or Network value guarantees dismissal or promotion.

## Procedure

1. Identify the current phase. In the Energy phase, list legal actions and costs. In the main-action phase, list the offered main actions, including the option to take none if available.
2. Carry this month's performance priority into both Energy and main-action comparisons. If it is unavailable, choose one from current evidence and the latest review, keeping missing history unknown. At the first Energy decision and again at the main action, answer briefly in the ordinary `take_action(notes=...)` rationale:
   - Which unresolved concern is supported by returned evidence, or is none supported? Improvement supports reassessment; strong statistics or one improved HR message do not clear every concern.
   - Which protective choice is offered and affordable in this phase, and which growth alternative competes with it? Compare their published directions for this concern and the benefit forgone. Include risk reduction even when a visible-stat gain is unneeded. Say when no offered option addresses the concern.
   - Why this action now? Normally prefer addressing a persistent serious warning over marginal growth. Explain an urgent Health or other survival override without assuming future recovery arrives before failure, and reconsider mitigation at the next legal opportunity.
   - What later available feedback about this concern would change the assessment within the remaining game? A prior Energy mitigation is an attempt, not the main phase's offered alternative or proof of clearance.
   Keep this as one compact concern summary: unresolved concern and latest relevant evidence; response taken and observed result or unknown; chosen tradeoff; feedback that would change the assessment. Reuse that summary at the main action, updating changed evidence rather than repeating the full history. Later Energy notes need only update a changed comparison or result. Silence, a phase transition, or an unrelated good statistic is not evidence of clearance; neither must all uncertainty disappear before reassessment. Use ordinary notes and permitted current-run context only; add no memory file or checkpoint-only call.

   If the returned warning persists after mitigation, carry it into the main-action comparison as unresolved. The fact that Energy was spent does not outweigh continuing adverse feedback. Comfortable Health or Dignity does not answer an organisational-risk warning. Prefer an offered action addressing a persistent serious concern over marginal growth unless a more urgent supported survival need displaces it. Reassess when later feedback changes; neither a quota nor indefinite mitigation without reassessment is required. Treat returned incidents as actual feedback, distinct from general handbook guidance.
3. Before the first Energy expenditure, compare useful allocations of this quarter's 3 points, using current offered actions, costs and effects. Compare a needed 2-point action plus a useful 1-point action with suitable 1-point combinations; this is a provisional comparison, not a commitment or an assumption that repetition stays available. Keep an urgent supported action affordable before buying marginal growth. For example, when 2-point defusal is warranted, do not spend two points elsewhere first unless a more urgent survival need justifies displacing it. One urgent recovery point can still leave two for defusal. Do not reserve points for a merely hypothetical concern or save them for another quarter. Recovery can protect future months; learning and intense work cost Health. Compare the pace of Health decline with the decisions before the next actual recovery opportunity, without inventing a safety cutoff. Defusal can be useful despite no published direct statistical effect.
4. After every Energy action, apply the actual-benefit check before choosing again. Use refreshed choices and remaining points, not the original sequence. If the intended gain was observed absent, put the relevant before/after values and the changed choice or separately supported reason for repetition in the next ordinary note. Preserve urgent supported two-point actions while affordable; compare remaining useful recovery, capability, relationships, delivery and protection rather than automatically escalating to intense work. Health's published upper bound is 10: ordinary recovery at 10 cannot add visible buffer. Compare another worthwhile legal affordable action, without automatically redirecting to Output; retain any separately supported protective purpose. Below 10, recent depletion and upcoming demands can justify further recovery without a fixed safe threshold or quota. If no worthwhile tolerable use remains, explain unused Energy. It expires; never force ineffective spending to reach zero.
5. For the separate main action, apply step 2's warning comparison to the current offered choices and evidence after Energy spending. A project, reporting, rotation, mentorship, relationship repair, or risk reduction may serve different needs. Choose one legal main action, or none when that is the soundest available choice; do not require mitigation every quarter or claim it guarantees safety.
6. After a returned review or promotion window, separate the grade from whether promotion occurred. Compare progress since the previous window: useful work actually completed, visibility of that work, collaboration, capability gained through stories/projects/learning, and responsibilities at the current or prospective level. A strong grade is not proof of readiness; a weak grade does not identify one cause. If advancement was missed or the same weakness persists, choose a different emphasis only where the evidence supports it. When evidence is broad, compare a manageable option that develops more than one relevant need instead of automatically buying more reporting or intense work. Reporting communicates work; it does not create completed work. Carry one supported development priority into the next month's ordinary checkpoint, with the result that would change it. Keep recent Health depletion, commitments, unresolved warnings and financial needs ahead of speculative promotion gains; do not compensate for uncertainty with unsafe workload, a fixed Skill target or a promised promotion date.

## Investment and opportunity cost

- Seek sustainable promotions through the weakness supported by review feedback: delivery, collaboration, or capability. Earlier promotion can improve later salary, but is not a directly purchasable action or guaranteed result of a project. Do not equate promotion following a project with proof that the project caused it.
- Compare affordable Energy sequences using current effects and actual results, including risk directions. Before intense work or learning, compare lower-cost alternatives and the Energy they leave. Repeated report preparation is preferable for an Output need only while returned results support the useful benefit; unchanged Output requires reassessment, even if the description still promises a gain. Do not respond by automatically escalating to intense work: compare its wellbeing and risk costs with recovery, protection and other development. No permanent amounts, availability or superior returns are assumed.
- Check capability at planning checkpoints and after a promotion or new task difficulty using current responsibilities, returned task feedback, recent Skill development and reviews. Higher levels have higher published requirements, but no numerical readiness threshold is known. No explicit capability warning means adequacy is unconfirmed, not deficient by default. Learning can serve a supported gap or valuable sustainable preparation; compare it with offered projects, mentorship or rotation that also develop capability, and count Skill gained through stories. Do not prescribe learning after every promotion, invent a level-to-Skill target, or wait for a dismissal warning. When capability evidence is reassuring and delivery or collaboration is the supported weakness, retain the Output/Network/Dignity emphasis.
- Health and Dignity each range from 0 to 10. Protect deteriorating buffers, while accounting for benefits unavailable at an upper bound. Recovery of a saturated visible statistic does not erase the possible value of a separately supported risk-reducing effect.
- For weak or falling Wealth, debt warnings, or prolonged financial strain, favour financially sustainable choices and avoid discretionary costs when suitable alternatives exist. Do not assume future salary, a bonus, or an uncertain promotion will arrive before insolvency. Financial and performance risks remain relevant even when organisational-risk feedback is reassuring.
- Respond to the first C/D review at the next legal opportunity. Use a stated weakness when supplied. If diagnosis is vague or absent, compare recent completed work and its visibility, collaboration feedback, task difficulties and capability development against current responsibilities. Keep the cause unknown where evidence is missing; a weak grade alone does not prove an Output, Network or Skill deficit. Choose a tolerable corrective action addressing the best-supported concern; when evidence does not distinguish a cause, prefer a useful balanced action with manageable costs. Reporting is appropriate for a visibility need, but is not an automatic cure or proof of completed work. Reassess with subsequent task results, warnings and the next actual review; do not wait for another weak grade to act. Compare consequential needs and costs rather than unrelated raw-stat sizes or inferred score-per-point ratios.

## Conflicts and edge cases

Use the current monthly performance priority from event-decisions when comparing Energy and main actions. If its expected progress did not appear, reconsider the action rather than repeating the plan automatically. The existing survival, warning, affordability and actual-result checks still govern. Monthly checkpoints add no Energy, main action, observation/log call solely for the checkpoint, or formal review outside the published phases. If an ending is returned, stop without another action or invented grade.

When Health is declining, compare learning or high-intensity work with recovery and less harmful development routes. When Output is high but Network or Dignity is falling, favour an action that addresses the neglected dimension if the weakness threatens future reviews or survival. When a promotion window approaches, strengthen Skill, Output, or Network where needed, but avoid sacrificing a fragile career for an uncertain promotion. Wealth and salary matter to the ending and prolonged negative Wealth is a published risk direction, yet no exact financial threshold is known.

## Constraints and contribution

Use only published action directions and current-run observations. Do not invent numerical action effects, hidden risk counters, promotion cutoffs, or scoring weights. If the current interface supplies a number, use it as current evidence, not a permanent rule. No reported visible cost is not proof of zero hidden risk. Do not assume fixed career phases or saved Energy. Keep notes brief and tie each claim to its evidence. This Skill converts the quarterly budget and main action into a rolling, evidence-based plan across the full run.
