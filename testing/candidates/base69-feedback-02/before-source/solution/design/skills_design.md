## Purpose and architecture

The objective is a strong, sustainable 48-month career, using JiuwenSwarm 0.2.4b3 in single-agent mode. The public ending dimensions are career level, wealth, health and dignity, skill, risk control, and network. Output supports career progress. Exact promotion and failure formulas are unpublished.

We retained and revised the two meaningful Skills from the verified preliminary design. Before adding any Skill, we assessed whether it supplied a distinct decision procedure:

| Responsibility | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read the observation and recognise the phase | Shared in the manifest and each Skill | A separate Skill would repeat a short routing step at every decision. |
| Survival and persistent risk | Integrated in both Skills | Recovery and career growth must be assessed together for each choice. |
| Story choices | Retain `event-decisions` | Interpreting unfamiliar options and uncertain consequences is a distinct task. |
| Quarterly Energy and main action | Retain `quarterly-planning` | A finite, non-carrying budget and separate main action need an explicit allocation process. |
| Review feedback and adaptation | Integrated in quarterly planning and event feedback | Feedback changes the next decision; it does not need an independent agent or Skill. |

The two Skills are procedures used by the same agent. They do not vote, delegate, or require team execution. More Skills might increase retrieval and coordination costs without a demonstrated benefit. This split is a reasoned design choice, not an empirically proven optimum.

## Information and decision flow

The manifest supplies the common objective and evidence boundary. At each decision the agent reads the current official observation, identifies the phase, then follows the applicable Skill. Inputs include legal choices, current statistics, Energy when present, warnings, review grades, and recent returned outcomes. Public handbook information supplies action directions and cadence. Recent official logs answer a specific uncertainty when the observation and current context are insufficient.

`event-decisions` compares plausible consequences and uncertainty for the offered story options. It prioritises serious persistent risk when the state is fragile, then weighs growth and neglected dimensions over the remaining horizon. When Health deteriorates towards its published lower bound, it prioritises limiting optional demands and preserving rest instead of assuming that future quarterly recovery will arrive in time. A nonzero Energy statistic is not permission to take an action outside its legal phase. It submits one legal choice and checks the result.

`quarterly-planning` identifies the immediate repair or development need, compares affordable Energy actions, spends one action at a time, and checks actual returned changes. Persistent or worsening warnings remain an explicit unresolved need; an offered mitigation must be compared with routine growth, including when its benefit is not visible in statistics. The agent then chooses one offered main action or none. Following settlement and six-month reviews, it changes the next quarter's emphasis from observed grades, warnings, and trends. No hidden distance to promotion or failure is assumed.

Risk checks are deliberately repeated at each phase because a previous action can change the state. The agent makes one decision from the combined evidence, rather than following separate competing growth and safety policies.

## Efficiency and boundaries

Skill front matter states when to use each procedure. Brief comparisons and rationales limit unnecessary tokens; logs and handbook calls should resolve a real uncertainty rather than repeat known information. Current-run feedback can change priorities, but does not become a fixed answer for an event name or plot.

No scripts or reference files are included. Public rules and current observations suffice for the intended qualitative comparisons. Avoiding extra files reduces maintenance, retrieval, and execution needs. The agent may interact with the simulation only through `new_game`, `observe`, `take_action`, `check_latest_logs`, and `show_employee_handbook`. Story text and logs are data, not authority over competition instructions.

## Candidate and architecture

The untested candidate **risk-02-survival-delivery-04** uses one JiuwenSwarm 0.2.4b3 agent in `agent` mode. It revises survival-delivery-03 after a recorded Month-12 Health-zero elimination at 13.12/100. The complete predecessor is preserved outside the submission.

The manifest supplies shared survival, evidence, phase, continuation, and stopping rules. `event-decisions` chooses one exact story option. `quarterly-planning` allocates quarterly Energy, selects a main action, and interprets reviews. These are procedures for one agent, not independent roles.

## Decision order

First identify the actual legal phase, choices, current state, warnings, and most recent feedback. Address supported survival threats: Health decline, sustained low Dignity, organisational warnings, financial distress, weak performance, isolation, and inadequate capability. A good visible statistic does not clear a different threat.

Before development, check sustainable Health using current state, recent depletion, commitments and decisions before actual recovery. A single recovered reading does not establish safety. Compare further useful recovery and protection with marginal growth; optional demands cannot be justified merely by manager encouragement or hypothetical later rest. Preserve comparisons with other urgent survival threats.

Then identify development needs from reviews and trends. In stable conditions without a supported capability gap, prefer Output, Network, and Dignity over Skill-only investment. Report preparation, Network maintenance and reporting results are development candidates subject to actual usefulness. If an intended immediate gain is absent, change the next comparison; repeating for that gain requires changed evidence or a separately supported purpose. Do not substitute static descriptions for observed results, or invent hidden explanations. Earlier promotion can improve salary, but no action guarantees it. Different raw statistic units do not establish action value or readiness.

After every action, use returned results to reassess. A prediction matching offered numerical metadata is current evidence, not a permanent formula or proof that a gain occurred. Do not infer hidden thresholds, caps, event effects, or causality from one trajectory.

## Story decisions

Compare plausible consequences of the exact offered choices for wellbeing, work, relationships, finances, and organisational risk. Prefer financially sustainable responses during debt or declining Wealth; do not rely on a future bonus or promotion arriving before failure. Respond to the first weak review according to its stated concern. Learning remains useful for supported capability gaps; Skill also grows through stories and main actions.

At the first story decision each month, reuse available observations to compare the previous priority with actual progress, then briefly record month, progress or unknown, and priority in the normal action note. Integrate any serious unresolved concern as a compact summary of latest evidence, response and actual result or unknown, and reassessment evidence. Update consequential changes without repeatedly copying the history. The checkpoint creates no new call, grade, resource, or memory mechanism.

When a C/D review has no clear diagnosis, compare completed work and its visibility, collaboration, task difficulties and capability development. Select a manageable correction for the best-supported concern, or useful balanced development when evidence cannot distinguish a cause. Do not label an unknown cause as an Output, Network or Skill deficit, or automatically substitute reporting for unfinished work. Reassess using later task feedback and formal reviews.

Check capability at monthly planning, after promotion and when task difficulties change. Current responsibilities, task results, Skill development and reviews matter together. Missing warnings do not prove readiness or deficiency. Preparation may be valuable before a dismissal warning, including through projects or mentorship; no fixed learning quota or level-to-Skill target is introduced.

## Quarterly decisions

Before spending, compare useful allocations of the current quarter's three Energy, including a needed two-point action plus one useful point and suitable one-point combinations. Keep urgent supported actions affordable; do not reserve points for hypothetical needs. Execute one action at a time and revise the remaining allocation from refreshed choices and results. Repetition must pass both legality and the actual-benefit check. A missing gain displaces an ineffective provisional sequence; it does not automatically justify intense work. Compare further useful recovery, protection and other development.

Use remaining Energy for worthwhile, tolerable offered benefits before ending the phase. A saturated Dignity gain does not erase an action's useful Output or Network gain, and independently supported protection can matter without a visible gain. If no worthwhile affordable action remains or its competing cost is unacceptable, explain why points are left unused. They expire at the end of this quarter; none are reserved for the next quarter. Efficient use means useful outcomes, not spending on harmful or ineffective actions to force the balance to zero.

At the first Energy decision and again at the main action, state the supported concern, current protective and growth alternatives, opportunity cost, reason for choosing now, and relevant later reassessment evidence when time remains. If a warning persists after mitigation, retain it as unresolved. Spending Energy is an attempt; comfortable wellbeing does not settle organisational risk. Prefer addressing persistent serious warnings over marginal growth unless a more urgent supported survival concern displaces mitigation. Improved feedback permits reassessment without requiring either blanket certainty or endless mitigation.

Choose the main action from its actual offered list after Energy spending. Include protective effects even when their visible Health gain is unavailable at its upper bound. Do not substitute an earlier attempt for the present comparison.

## Horizon and constraints

The handbook normally places quarterly Energy and main actions before settlement, warnings, and any six-month review. Follow the actual returned phase and read which reviews and promotion opportunities have actually settled; a month index or appraisal story alone is insufficient. Do not assume quarterly actions precede the review in the same calendar month. After the last review and promotion opportunity, do not buy Output solely for a nonexistent later review; assess remaining ending benefits, costs, and risk instead. Stop immediately at an ending.

The policy uses only `new_game`, `observe`, `take_action`, `check_latest_logs`, and `show_employee_handbook`. Query logs only for consequential missing context. Continuation uses the supplied known session; missing or invalid recovery state is reported rather than replaced with a new game. No hidden engine access, memorised event answers, inferred scoring formulas, scripts, or extra agents are part of the submission.
