## Further execution improvements

The predecessor's new run loaded both matching Skills but repeatedly treated Health 6 as safe despite prior depletion. It accepted optional demands using future recovery or review opportunity as justification. Six of its nine Energy points went to four report preparations and two Network maintenance actions without the intended visible gains in the immediate returned observations; Dignity was already full. All nine points were spent. Repeated reporting was rationalised as a correction for a visibility-only weakness that the broad C-review feedback did not establish. These observations show execution failures, not proof of an engine defect, a hidden cap or a unique cause of every poor outcome.

The revision requires current-run results to change the next choice when an intended immediate benefit is absent. Repetition for that gain must have changed relevant evidence or a separately supported purpose; static metadata alone is insufficient. Recovery and the protective main action must remain candidates when depletion is concerning even after some recovery. Current responsibilities and actual task/review feedback are required before capability-readiness claims. No named-event answer, fixed Health buffer, compulsory recovery allocation or permanent blacklist is introduced.

The current-quarter budget is compared before the first Energy purchase so marginal development does not accidentally make a needed two-point action unaffordable. A warranted two-point action can be combined with one useful point; three one-point actions remain candidates when they better address present needs. Neither pattern is mandatory. Plans are provisional and rechecked after every action. Remaining useful affordable benefits should be taken before points expire; harmful or ineffective spending is not required merely to use all three points. A choice can remain valuable for Output, Network or supported protection when its Dignity benefit is saturated.

A weak review without a diagnosis now triggers a comparison of actual work completion and visibility, collaboration, task difficulties and capability progress. The cause remains uncertain where the evidence is insufficient. A manageable correction is selected without waiting for another weak review; later feedback tests the assessment. Reporting is not assumed to resolve all poor performance.

Capability checks consider rising responsibilities and current task feedback before a dismissal warning, while recognising Skill gained through stories and main actions. Neither a promotion nor missing warnings automatically requires learning. The policy retains its default development emphasis and has no fixed readiness threshold or learning schedule.

Ordinary notes carry a compact concern summary with latest evidence, response and actual result or unknown, and what would change the assessment. Consequential missing gains include relevant before/after values when available. The summary is updated at relevant checkpoints or consequential changes, replacing repeated history. No external memory, extra checkpoint calls, or hidden state is introduced. The new checks address observed failures of the predecessor's earlier general safeguards; whether they change behaviour remains untested.

## Interpreting metrics

Ending weights and contributions describe different components on different scales. A raw career-level point is not as easy to obtain as a raw Skill point; promotions require sustained multifactorial performance. Raw-point conversion does not establish return per action. Two equal risk values with equal zero contributions do not establish a slope or show that risk control is worthless. Limited observations do not establish uncapped returns or a universal capability threshold.

The submission therefore uses public meanings, salary, legal choices, current offered effects, and current-run feedback. It does not encode inferred ending formulas, hidden thresholds, caps, event-specific mappings, or score-per-point optimisation. Skill remains relevant to reviews, promotion, failure risk, and the ending. Network and Output deserve investment when they are supported constraints; Dignity and Health need protection without automatic spending on unavailable gains at their upper bounds.
