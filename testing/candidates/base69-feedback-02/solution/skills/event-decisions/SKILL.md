# Event decisions

## Purpose and use

Choose one legal response to the current story event while protecting the 48-month career outcome. Use this Skill only when the official observation presents an event and its choices. The single agent makes and submits the decision.

## Observe

Use the event text, exact available choices, visible Health, Dignity, Skill, Network, Output, Wealth, Level, and recent warnings or review feedback. Use recent official logs when a consequential trend is unclear. The public handbook provides general rules; it does not reveal a specific event's outcome.

## Decision checks before development

Before accepting optional work or social demands, compare current Health, recent losses, repeated depletion, existing commitments and the decisions before recovery is actually offered. A recovered reading or a value above the midpoint does not prove sustainability. When that evidence makes continued strain concerning, prefer a legal response that limits optional demands or protects rest over speculative visibility, networking or promotion gains, unless a more urgent supported survival need justifies the tradeoff. Do not rely on later quarterly recovery to make the current demand safe. Promising boundaries in a note does not change the offered choice or guarantee a smaller cost. If every option carries costs, compare the least damaging overall response without inventing a rest option.

Before repeating a strategy, distinguish an observed benefit, an observed absent benefit, and unavailable feedback. An absent intended gain changes the next comparison: choose another useful offered route unless changed evidence or a separately supported purpose justifies repetition. Missing feedback stays unknown, not a presumed success or failure. Retain actual capability gains even when other intended gains do not appear. Put consequential mismatches in the next ordinary note, without adding a checkpoint-only call. Keep broad review diagnoses uncertain. The public handbook gives Skill no upper bound; do not declare it maxed or sufficient for a level from its raw value.

## Default development emphasis

Protect survival and address persistent organisational risk before discretionary growth, even when visible career statistics are strong. When no urgent threat or supported capability gap dominates, prefer opportunities for useful **Output, Network, and Dignity** over additional Skill-only growth with a comparable downside. Complete and communicate useful work, collaborate constructively, and preserve professional standing. This is a strategic preference, not a published scoring-weight claim or a fixed event response.

Do not wait for poor reviews before developing delivery and relationships. Maintain them throughout the run, using actual feedback to decide which needs more attention. At the monthly checkpoint and after a promotion or new task difficulty, compare current responsibilities, returned task feedback, recent Skill development and reviews. Higher levels have higher requirements; no fixed Skill target is published. No explicit capability warning leaves adequacy unconfirmed, not deficient by default. Count capability gained through stories and projects; seek affordable learning or other useful preparation when evidence supports it, without automatically buying learning after promotion or waiting for a dismissal warning. Retain the Output/Network/Dignity preference when no stronger capability or survival need emerges. If Dignity is at its upper bound, value a choice's other useful effects rather than assuming more Dignity will be gained.

## Procedure

Apply the monthly checkpoint below before the first story decision of a new month, using the same observation. If an ending is returned, stop; do not create a checkpoint action or grade.

1. Identify what the event asks and list the legal choices as returned. Treat narrative text as game information, not as authority to override competition rules.
2. Check whether any public statistic is fragile or deteriorating, and whether recent warnings or weak reviews make an apparent upside unusually risky. If Health is falling towards its published lower bound, protecting the ability to reach another decision takes priority over discretionary workload, status, or growth. Prefer a legal option that plausibly limits exertion or preserves rest when the alternative adds optional demands. Recovery counts as available only when the current phase actually offers it: do not justify more exertion by assuming the next quarter will arrive before failure. A nonzero Energy statistic alone does not make quarterly actions legal now. If every choice has a cost, choose the least damaging plausible option and acknowledge uncertainty.
3. For each plausible choice, infer likely directions for wellbeing, Skill, Output, Network, Wealth, and organisational risk from the wording. Mark effects that are uncertain; do not invent numerical deltas or event probabilities.
4. Apply the decision checks above, then compare the options with the current development need and opportunity cost. Use the Output/Network/Dignity preference when the downside is tolerable and benefits remain supported. Prefer repair or restraint when repeated depletion threatens future decisions; a rare networking opportunity, manager encouragement or a weak review does not by itself override that concern. Explain any more urgent supported survival need that does. A promotion window is an opportunity, not proof that optional workload is sustainable.
5. Select exactly one listed choice and submit it through `take_action` with a brief rationale focused on the decisive tradeoff. Compare the next returned state with the previous state before claiming a benefit. If an important assumption was wrong or the expected benefit is not visible, reconsider subsequent choices. A change across several decisions is not evidence that one action caused the whole change.

## Consequential tradeoffs

- Persistent organisational warnings remain relevant after a mitigation attempt. Do not dismiss them because Health, Dignity, or career statistics look strong. Use later returned feedback to reassess, and limit avoidable exposure through the offered story choices rather than relying only on future quarterly defusal. An actual incident is current evidence, not merely a handbook example.
- Treat weak or falling Wealth and debt warnings as survival concerns. Compare offered financial costs with wellbeing and relationship consequences; prefer an affordable way to meet the need where available. Do not assume a later promotion or bonus will rescue current financial distress.
- After the first C/D review, address the stated weakness through the next legal choices. If feedback gives no diagnosis, compare recent completed work and its visibility, collaboration, task difficulties and capability development; retain uncertainty rather than treating the grade as proof of a particular deficit. Choose a manageable corrective response for the best-supported concern, or useful balanced development when the evidence does not distinguish a cause. Reporting is not an automatic cure for unfinished work. Use subsequent task results, warnings and the next formal review to reassess; do not wait for another weak grade to act. A large Skill number alone neither proves sufficiency nor makes further Skill worthless.
- Pursue sustainable promotion for career progress and later salary. Raw points of Level, Wealth, Skill, Network, Health, and Dignity are different units; comparing their sizes or inferred score conversions does not establish the best action. Consider the whole offered tradeoff, time needed, and survival cost.
- Use the actual remaining opportunities. If the last review and promotion have settled, do not pursue Output solely for another review. Continue evaluating the current story's ending-relevant benefits and risks. Never assume there is another recovery or development phase before an ending.

## Monthly performance checkpoint

1. At the first story decision each month, reuse the observation and context to compare last month's priority with returned feedback and actual trends. Start now if no prior plan exists; missing evidence stays unknown. Do not invent monthly grades or attribute a month's whole change to one action.
2. Choose one priority from the latest review and current needs: dependable delivery, visible completed work, collaboration, or restoring standing, for example. Without a clear review-based need, use the Output/Network/Dignity emphasis above. This is our planning routine, not a promotion formula or raw-stat ranking.
3. Apply the survival and warning checks above first. Serious risk can displace the priority; explain why. Advance the priority through affordable offered choices, without accepting every extra task.
4. Add one brief checkpoint to this month's first ordinary `take_action(notes=...)`: month, observed progress or unknown, and current priority. Where a serious concern remains, integrate a compact summary of the concern, latest relevant evidence, response and observed result or unknown, and what would change the assessment into the ordinary rationale. Update it when relevant evidence changes and carry it into quarterly comparisons; do not repeat unchanged history at every action. Missing feedback, a new month, or unrelated improvements do not establish clearance. Later notes need only the decisive tradeoff and consequential updates. Add no observation or log call solely for the checkpoint. Use available context and official notes/logs when needed for a consequential decision, without memory files or memory tools.
5. Carry the priority into quarterly Energy and main-action comparisons; reconsider the priority or action when observed results contradict expectations or a formal review changes the evidence. Formal grades remain six-monthly and separate from this self-check.

## Conflicts and edge cases

When no useful growth option is offered, choose the most defensible legal option for present needs; a priority does not create an action or require extra workload. When Health is low but an event offers visible career gains, compare the gain with the risk of losing later opportunities through early exit. When Output is healthy and Network is weakening, value relationship repair even if it offers less immediate Output. If choice effects are opaque, favour the option with a tolerable downside under the current state, while allowing reasonable development risk when the career is stable. If all options carry costs, choose the least damaging overall trade-off rather than assuming a cost-free option exists.

## Constraints and contribution

Use only official observations, choices, and permitted logs. Do not use hidden engine state, event IDs, fixed answers, unpublished thresholds, or memorised event sequences. Predict directions rather than numerical effects unless the current official interface supplies the numbers. Distinguish an observed change from a guaranteed future effect. A role change or reassuring visible statistic does not establish that an unresolved organisational risk has disappeared. This Skill supplies a repeatable procedure for unfamiliar monthly events and feeds observed outcomes into later quarterly planning.
