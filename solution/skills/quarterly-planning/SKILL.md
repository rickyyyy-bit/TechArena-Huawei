# Quarterly planning

## Purpose

Build a strong, sustainable career across the 48-month simulation. Prioritise **avoiding early dismissal and achieving sustained reviews and promotions**, then improve the ending dimensions without wasting scarce actions. Skill, Network, and Output all inform promotion; higher levels also earn higher published salaries. Manage organisational risk proactively but do not abandon career growth whenever a concern appears.

This Skill handles **quarterly Energy, quarterly main actions, and interpretation of actual six-month reviews**. `event-decisions` handles monthly story choices. The same single agent makes all decisions.

## Observe and determine the priority

Use the current legal phase and offered actions, Energy, month, Level, CurrentLevelDuration, Health, Dignity, Skill, Network, Output, Wealth, the latest review and promotion result, HR/organisational warnings, and recent returned changes where available. Reuse information already returned; request official logs only when missing information could change a consequential decision. Unknown history remains unknown.

Choose the current priority from evidence, not the lowest raw statistic:

- `FOUNDATION`: Before the first formal review, build **Skill, Network, and Output together** so performance is credible from the beginning. Do not spend early quarters protecting already-strong visible statistics while fundamental career development is missing.
- `DELIVERY`: Review or trends indicate weak completed work or visibility; improve dependable results and communication.
- `RELATIONSHIPS`: Review or trends indicate weak collaboration, isolation, or deteriorating Network.
- `CAPABILITY`: Feedback indicates a technical gap; or, while other growth directions have repeatedly failed to materialise, useful Skill development remains available.
- `RISK`: Organisational feedback indicates a consequential unresolved concern.
- `FINANCIAL`: Savings or financial warnings indicate a credible threat to remaining employed.
- `RECOVERY`: Health or Dignity is fragile or deteriorating enough to threaten future decisions.
- `BALANCED`: No clear overriding concern; seek useful, sustainable progress across dimensions.

Priorities may coexist. Address urgent threats without neglecting the performance foundations needed for the next review. At a formal review, update the priority; do not fabricate interim grades.

## Decision order

1. **Check immediate failure directions:** credible organisational warnings, financial distress, repeated weak reviews, extreme or declining Health/Dignity, persistent isolation, or a demonstrated Skill gap. Give attention in proportion to severity and time until the next opportunity to respond. There is no published exact cutoff.
2. **Secure performance and advancement:** choose investments likely to improve review readiness and sustained Skill, Network, and Output. Prioritise a strong foundation before the first review; thereafter respond to the actual bottleneck.
3. **Avoid ineffective spending:** before every Energy expenditure, ask whether its intended benefit is still needed and whether similar recent actions produced an observable change. Repeated nonresponse is reason to **reconsider**, not proof of an unpublished cap or level-specific gate. Consider any other published benefit of the action before dropping it.
4. **Maintain proportionate safeguards:** preserve sufficient Health and Dignity, and act on risk feedback, without treating perfect visible statistics as the objective.
5. **Update after feedback:** read the returned state, check remaining legal options and Energy, and adjust the next action. Never infer a guaranteed numerical effect or internal threshold from a development run.

## HR warnings and cumulative organisational risk

Treat the **meaning and trend of the HR partner's current-run message** as useful evidence. Distinguish clearly reassuring feedback, mild or ambiguous concern, persistent adverse feedback, and urgently negative or worsening warnings. These are reasoning categories, not official risk levels.

- **Reassuring and stable:** normally favour career development. A discretionary risk action needs a separate credible reason, such as unresolved consequential conflict.
- **Concern or unclear improvement:** examine recent developments, compare `Defuse legacy risk` with near-term performance needs, and favour lower-risk growth choices. Do not interpret a mitigation attempt as clearance.
- **Persistently adverse or escalating:** give risk reduction high priority when offered and affordable. Reconsider mitigation at the next legal opportunity if later feedback remains adverse; one attempt may not be sufficient. Avoid adding optional conflict or heavy workload where safer progress is available.
- **Urgent warning:** prioritise an available credible response, while maintaining essential performance and professional obligations.

`Defuse legacy risk` has **no published direct visible-stat effect**; this is not evidence of zero value, nor a guarantee of clearance. `Lay low and reduce risk` publicly improves Health. If the **current offered action description** explicitly identifies an additional risk-related effect, it may be considered as current-run evidence, without assuming its magnitude or that future descriptions are identical.

Do not spend on automatic risk-defusal schedules, repeated action quotas, or inferred hidden counters. Reassess warnings and recent consequences rather than counting past mitigation actions.

## Use the quarterly Energy budget efficiently

The published quarterly budget is **3 Energy, unused points do not carry over**. Only act in the legal Energy phase. Inspect the exact offered choices and affordability before each action, then inspect the resulting state before the next choice.

| Energy action | Cost | Public direction | When to consider it |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Prepare report materials | 1 | Output +, Dignity + | Output/visible delivery is needed **and the likely benefit is still useful**; reconsider if repeated attempts have no relevant result. |
| Network maintenance | 1 | Network +, Dignity + | Relationships or Network need strengthening **and attempts remain useful**; reconsider after repeated nonresponse. |
| Skill learning | 2 | Skill +, Health - | A meaningful capability need and adequate Health; compare opportunity cost with available story/main-action development. Not an automatic default. |
| High-intensity work | 2 | Output +, Health -, Dignity - | Important delivery need when the added burden and organisational uncertainty are tolerable; compare cheaper alternatives. |
| Health recovery | 1 | Health + | Health is below its published maximum and recovery is useful for an actual or foreseeable problem. |
| Mental recovery | 1 | Dignity + | Dignity is below its published maximum and recovery is useful for an actual or foreseeable problem. |
| Defuse legacy risk | 2 | No published direct visible-stat effect | Current warnings, unresolved conflict or credible risk exposure justify mitigation. |

**Pre-spend check:** `What is the current priority? What does this action improve? Did a comparable recent action produce that benefit? Would another affordable action address a more urgent need?` A repeated lack of visible improvement suggests shifting resources, but do not assume the cause is a hidden ceiling, or ignore an action's other effects.

In stable periods, reports and networking are good candidates **only while they address needs that can still improve**. If Output and Network have stopped responding to relevant investments but Skill continues developing, give more weight to capability-supporting opportunities and defensible risk mitigation rather than automatically repeating ineffective actions. Reassess if promotion, feedback, or observable returns change. Do not assume a particular level has fixed caps or that Skill alone unlocks promotion.

Spend one legal action at a time using the exact choice returned. Consider a beneficial affordable action before the phase ends, but do not spend solely to exhaust Energy.

## Quarterly main action

Select **one** offered main action (or none if offered and defensible), using the state **after** Energy spending. Pair it with the current priority and test whether its relevant effects have remained useful.

- **Apply for a key project** (`Output +`, `Skill +`, `Network +`): strong candidate when multiple dimensions need development and the workload is manageable. If some benefits repeatedly fail to appear, still assess whether its remaining effects justify the commitment; do not repeat by habit.
- **Report results to your manager** (`Output +`, `Network +`, `Dignity +`): useful for delivery, visibility and relationships when relevant improvements remain possible.
- **Showcase results** (`Output +`, `Dignity +`) or **Fight for resources** (`Output +`, `Network +`): consider when work visibility or resourcing is a genuine need; weigh reputational consequences.
- **Build a mentorship** or **Apply for rotation** (`Skill +`, `Network +`): consider when capability development remains valuable, especially if other growth actions are no longer producing useful results.
- **Repair a key relationship** (`Network +`, `Dignity +`): select when a real relationship concern or deteriorating Network supports it.
- **Lay low and reduce risk** (`Health +` in public rules): consider for Health or when current legal action wording provides relevant risk-reduction evidence; weigh the missed growth action.
- **No action**: select only when offered and preferable to the real costs of committing.

Do not assume any main action is always best, or dismiss an untried alternative merely because earlier tests used another one.

## Reviews, promotion and the remaining horizon

At every **actual six-month review**, check grade (S/A/B/C/D), bonus, Level change, warnings, and growth trends. Promotion considers sustained Skill, Network and Output, with increasing requirements at higher levels; the exact assessment is unpublished.

- **Before the first review:** actively develop all three promotion-relevant dimensions, subject to urgent threats. Do not mistake cautious maintenance for a sufficient starting career foundation.
- **C or D / worsening feedback:** address the specific performance concern immediately. If several dimensions are weak, favour opportunities helping multiple foundations. Keep delivery and relationships credible even while mitigating risk.
- **B or better / no promotion:** sustain what is working, investigate the evidence-supported gap, and avoid inventing a promotion threshold.
- **Repeated nonresponse in Output/Network:** reallocate effort to genuinely useful dimensions or protection, while still maintaining competent delivery. Revisit this assessment after new evidence or a promotion.
- **Later career:** prefer benefits that can still matter before the ending; protect earned progress and avoid unnecessary last-minute organisational escalation. An offered final action can still matter if the simulation has not ended.

Higher levels earn higher published monthly salaries, so earlier **sustainable** advancement may also support Wealth. If savings deteriorate, avoid unnecessary financial losses and preserve the possibility of a later review or salary increase; do not assume recovery will arrive in time.

## Execution and fair play

Use only the five official MCP tools: `new_game`, `observe`, `take_action`, `check_latest_logs`, and `show_employee_handbook`. Only use the current run's observations, legal choices, permitted logs, and public handbook. No engine files, hidden data, memorised events, inferred numerical ceilings, private scoring formulas, or unpublished failure and promotion thresholds. Never create extra phases, reviews, actions or agents.

Keep `take_action` notes short: **priority and decisive evidence; expected direction and main trade-off**. Do not add calls just to restate the strategy. Stop if the game ends.
