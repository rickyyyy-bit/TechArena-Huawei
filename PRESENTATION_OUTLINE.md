## 1. Reasoning while designing the Skills

We wanted a sustainable 48-month career, with growth across the ending dimensions. We retained two Skills because story interpretation and quarterly resource allocation have different decision structures. Risk assessment is part of both. Phase recognition and review adaptation are integrated instead of becoming additional overlapping Skills. One agent makes all decisions.

## 2. What earlier logs revealed and what changed

Our baseline run reached Month 36 at L4 before a connection failure interrupted it without an ending score. Its logs showed risk warnings acknowledged without mitigation, and numerical benefits asserted without support from the next observation. We made the mitigation comparison and observed-effect checks explicit. The next version failed through Health reaching zero in Month 3, scoring 21.77 locally: the agent kept accepting optional demands because it assumed quarterly recovery would arrive in time. We therefore added a general rule to protect the ability to reach recovery, rather than rely on a future recovery phase.

Tested v3 then completed all 48 months, ending at L5 with Health and Dignity both 10 and a local quantitative score of 64.52 out of 100. That is one successful local completion. Different event paths prevent us from claiming the instruction change caused the improvement or that the result is consistent. The agent still sometimes overstates numerical effects and skips the intended mitigation comparison. Those are unresolved limitations, not fixes we claim succeeded.

V3's six-month grades were C, C, B, B, B, B, A and A; promotions occurred in Months 12, 18, 30 and 48. It chose key projects in 14 of 16 quarters and spent 25 Energy preparing reports, sometimes continuing to predict Output increases that were not observed. Network was already 36 in Months 36 and 42 without L5: Network alone does not explain promotion timing. Sustained results, Skill, Network and Output matter, while exact thresholds are unpublished. These observations motivate earlier feedback checks, not mandatory variety or a numerical target.

The separate v4 experiment changed several behaviours and scored 42.12, ending through a trust-related failure in Month 39 despite high Health and Dignity. Its notes/log transport worked, while omissions, stale fields and skipped observations remained. These individual trajectories do not isolate causes. The monthly candidate retains v3's safeguards; new risk systems, operating bands, Network targets and token redesign remain separate experiments.

The candidate has an eight-case instruction desk review, not model-playtest evidence. After explicit paid-test authorisation, repeat unchanged v3 runs before repeated frozen-candidate runs. Record source commit/hashes, actual monthly adherence, completion/failure category, grades, promotion months, quantitative score, tokens per survival month and reported cost. A first run would show adherence and one trajectory's outcome; stronger reviews or promotion beyond L5 remain unproven.

## 3. Why scripts and reference files were omitted

The decisions use the current observation, public handbook, and current-run feedback. Short prose procedures suffice for our intended comparisons. Additional executable files would introduce complexity without a demonstrated benefit. We did not include event tables or private formulas.

## 4. Adaptation to changed settings

The agent reads the current phase, available actions, costs, warnings, and review feedback. It compares the current tradeoffs rather than recognising named events. It uses current public rules and avoids assuming hidden thresholds. This is intended to tolerate changed wording and ordering. One completed local run gives execution evidence, but robustness across changed settings and repeated runs remains unproven.

## 5. Agent and Skills: contribution and limits

Skills give the agent an explicit decision routine and reminders about neglected dimensions and long-term consequences. The agent still interprets incomplete information and chooses among uncertain options. Skills cannot reveal hidden conditions, guarantee survival, or replace evidence from actual play. Concision helps efficiency only if the model continues to follow the essential checks.
